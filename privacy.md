# Lolly Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

**Short version: Lolly does not collect your data.** We have no accounts, no servers that receive your content, no analytics and no advertising or tracking.

## What stays on your device
Your chats, memories, documents you add, pictures and storyboards are stored on your iPhone. They are encrypted with keys kept in your device's Keychain, protected by device file protection, and excluded from backups. Lolly's developer cannot see them.

## The one network use: model downloads
When you choose to download an offline chat model, Lolly fetches the model files from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). The request contains only the model file path, not your content. Hugging Face may log standard connection data such as your IP address under its own policy.

## Microphone, speech, camera and photos
- Voice features use your microphone and Apple's speech recognition on your device. Audio is not sent to us. Voices you record for Storyboard bubbles are stored encrypted on your device and are deleted with the storyboard.
- Camera and photo access is used only when you import something to read on-device text. You can decline. In Storyboard the camera takes a photo for a character; it is cut out on your device and not saved to your photo library.

## Optional picture features (Apple Image Playground)
If you turn on Settings > Illustrations, Draw it and Storyboard open Apple's Image Playground. Lolly passes a short picture idea and keeps no copy outside your saved pictures. Apple may process the request on your device or in Apple's Private Cloud Compute, under Apple's privacy terms. Lolly adds no server of its own. If you choose a photo or drawing to make a Storyboard character, Lolly cuts out the subject on your device with Apple's Vision framework and saves it encrypted on your device; it is not uploaded. These features are off until you switch them on.

## iCloud
Sync is not switched on in this version. Your data stays on this device.

## TestFlight
If you test through TestFlight, Apple provides us with crash reports and feedback you choose to send. Lolly itself collects nothing.

## Children
Lolly is not directed to children under 13.

## Your control
Delete a chat to delete its pictures, delete individual pictures or storyboards, or erase everything in Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data.
