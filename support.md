# Lolly support

Contact: [wavypix@gmail.com](mailto:wavypix@gmail.com)

## FAQ

**What do I need?** An iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Chat works offline on any supported iPhone once a model is downloaded.

**How do I download a model?** Open the model picker in a chat or the welcome screens and tap Download. Models are about 1 to 3 GB, so use Wi-Fi. Downloads continue if you leave the app.

**The picture features are missing.** Draw it and Storyboard use Apple's Image Playground, which needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (15 Pro or later) turned on, and you must switch them on in Settings > Illustrations.

**Where are my chats and pictures?** Only on your phone, encrypted. Deleting a chat deletes its pictures. Settings has an option to erase everything.

**Lolly is not a doctor or a crisis service.** If you or someone else is in danger, contact local emergency services. In the US you can call or text 988.

**Send feedback.** Settings > Credits > Send feedback, or email the address above. In TestFlight you can also screenshot and tap Share Beta Feedback.
